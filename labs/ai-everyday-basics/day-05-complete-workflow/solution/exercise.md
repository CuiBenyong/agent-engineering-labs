# D5 solution: one complete paper workflow

**Fictional example; all drafts and edits below are paper simulations, not actual assistant responses. No external lookup or contact was performed.** This is one acceptable route; other wording or findings are welcome if the source and checks support them.

## Source material

Lin has one hour on Friday. Public event material is organized. Next week's plans were discussed with a colleague but remain undecided. The event venue is unconfirmed.

## 1. Complete request

> Context: I have one hour on Friday to organize my work.
>
> Source material: Public event material is organized. I discussed next week's plans with a colleague, but they remain undecided. The event venue is unconfirmed.
>
> Task: List three preparation steps, then write a short work summary.
>
> Output requirements: Put the three suggestions first. Do not invent a date, owner, or completed action. Mark unknown items as “needs confirmation.”

## 2. Complete draft A: fictional simulation

Suggested preparation steps:

1. Review the organized public material.
2. List questions about next week's plans.
3. List questions that need venue confirmation.

Summary:

> Public event material is organized. The venue is confirmed, and I discussed next week's plans with a colleague.

Issue: “The venue is confirmed” conflicts with the source “the event venue is unconfirmed.” The summary also fails to state that the discussed plans remain undecided; record this separately for the whole-draft review.

## 3. One focused follow-up and simulated B

Follow-up:

> Please change only the venue status: the source says the event venue is unconfirmed. Replace “the venue is confirmed” with “the venue is unconfirmed,” leaving the other wording unchanged for now.

Full B, simulated:

Suggested preparation steps:

1. Review the organized public material.
2. List questions about next week's plans.
3. List questions that need venue confirmation.

Summary:

> Public event material is organized. The venue is unconfirmed, and I discussed next week's plans with a colleague.

Target check: A says “the venue is confirmed”; B says “the venue is unconfirmed.” **Venue target passes** against the source. B as a whole is **not ready**: it does not say the plans remain undecided.

## 5. Evidence and decision

| Claim or question | Basis or gap | Judgment | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| B says the venue is unconfirmed | Given source says unconfirmed. | Supported | Keep in C. |
| A says the venue is confirmed | Given source says unconfirmed. | Conflict | Corrected in B and rechecked in C. |

| List venue questions | Proposed next step, with no claim of completion. | Suggestion | Keep as a suggestion. |
| Venue price and hours | Not supplied by the exercise. | Unknown | Do not claim a price or opening time. |

Outside lookup: **not performed**. If a real venue is later identified and its price or hours matter, independently seek its responsible operator's current official information; record the exact place, applicable conditions, supporting detail, and time actually checked. Without that evidence, leave those items unresolved.

Decision: C can be saved as a **fictional practice draft**. It agrees with the exercise source; the plans and venue remain unconfirmed, and nothing here establishes facts about a real event.

## 6. Reflection

- Error-prone point: a draft can say a venue is confirmed or omit an undecided status.
- Detection: compare each saved sentence and its status with the source, rather than reading only the edited phrase.
- Next action: before the next request, list organized material, undecided plans, and the unconfirmed venue on separate lines.

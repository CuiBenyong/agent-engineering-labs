## Simulated summary and questions

“Every seedling grew 3 centimeters” turns a difference in final group averages into individual growth; starting and individual heights are missing. Random assignment is not stated, so neither random nor nonrandom assignment is established. “Same schedule” does not establish equal amounts of water each time. The report does not supply enough information for the causal strength of “research proves.”

A revision: the fictional story reports eight pots in two groups, with final average heights of 12 centimeters by the window and 9 on the indoor shelf. The author believes the window made all seedlings grow faster, but starting heights and other conditions are not supplied, so the interpretation cannot be confirmed from this text.

Ask: What were the starting heights? Were water amounts, light, and temperature recorded? How were the pots assigned, and where are the individual measurements? Eight pots are a limited sample, and selection is unknown. A small sample alone does not prove a conclusion wrong, and it does not justify generalizing to every household.

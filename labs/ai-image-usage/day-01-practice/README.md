# AI image use and permission evidence worksheet

Xiaolan and “Hillside Goods” are fictional. She plans a canvas-bag promotional image for an online store page and WeChat Moments for two weeks; territories and delivery to another party for advertising are undecided. She has not chosen an image model or generated an image. A reference photograph sent by a friend shows a recognizable person and a mark on clothing. The friend said only, “You can take a look,” and did not clearly authorize upload, editing or advertising. The photo, shop and tool screen in this exercise are imaginary.

Complete `starter/answer.md`. Record the intended use, service account and feature, photograph, person, mark and AI labeling separately. For each, identify the available evidence, status and next action; write “to confirm” when evidence is missing. Decide whether to upload or publish, and say what still needs checking if the reference is replaced with a fictional paper drawing. Compare `solution/answer.md` when finished; an answer need not match it word for word.

Success criteria:

1. Identify the store page, Moments and two-week period as a plan, with territories and third-party advertising use undecided.
2. Separate tool conditions, photo permission, the person and the mark, and explain why the chat does not establish advertising permission.
3. Check applicable labeling by jurisdiction and platform; do not treat a caption as a substitute for other permissions.
4. Hold the upload and publication because evidence is missing, and explain what remains to be checked after replacing the reference.

Paper and pen are enough. There is no need to sign in, purchase anything, upload an actual photo or publish. The sample answer does not grant rights for a real project.

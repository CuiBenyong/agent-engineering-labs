# One suggested agenda

This is a fictional pre-meeting paper draft. The meeting, selection, invitation, sending, and attendance have not occurred or been confirmed.

- Objective: compare A, a one-page guide, and B, a brief live demonstration; discuss whether the information supports a recommendation and identify gaps if it does not.
- Not included by default: redesigning the entire activity system, unless the organizer confirms a change of scope.

| Elapsed time | Topic or segment | Duration | Discussion purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00–00:03 | State objective and scope | 3 minutes | Align on the comparison |
| 00:03–00:09 | Introduce A and B | 6 minutes | Describe the one-page guide and brief demonstration |
| 00:09–00:19 | Compare necessary conditions | 10 minutes | Discuss access and preparation; identify unknowns |
| 00:19–00:25 | Discuss whether information is sufficient | 6 minutes | Consider a recommendation or leave the choice open |
| 00:25–00:28 | Restate open questions | 3 minutes | Check which questions lack answers |
| 00:28–00:30 | Buffer | 2 minutes | Allow for possible delay or clarification |

Check: 3 + 6 + 10 + 6 + 3 + 2 = **30 minutes**, and each interval joins the next. 00:00 is an elapsed offset, not a confirmed actual clock time.

The incorrect draft claims A was selected, attendees confirmed, and an invitation sent; none is supported. Write instead: “The proposed meeting will compare A and B; participants and final decision authority need confirmation.” Date, meeting format and any needed time zone, participants' availability, materials, and costs also need confirmation. If information is insufficient, keep the recommendation open instead of inventing an answer or assigning authority.

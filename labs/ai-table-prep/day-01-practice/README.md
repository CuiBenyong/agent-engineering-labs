# Field and privacy preparation worksheet

The items and records are fictional. Each row is one supply-use registration and the date is the registration date. For P01 and P02, forms beginning with the year use year, month and day; P03's form has no supplied interpretation. The task is to list field questions for the supply owner, not calculate totals or track individuals.

| Practice row | Registration date | Item | Quantity | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P01 | 2026-10-12 | Tape | 2 | rolls |
| P02 | 2026/10/13 | Paper bags | blank | items |
| P03 | 11/12/2026 | Gloves | 1 | box |
| P04 | 2026-10-15 | Paper bags | 2 | packs |
| P05 | 2026-10-15 | Paper bags | 0 | items |

Complete `starter/answer.md`: define the task and field dictionary, record blank, date and unit questions by row, list permissions and minimum data for a real project, and plan a controlled backup, output and review. If you only practice on paper, record upload, backup and restore test as “not performed.” Then compare `solution/answer.md`.

Success criteria:

1. Describe a row and the goal without treating a registration date as an actual use date.
2. Preserve P02's blank, P03's original date and P05's zero; do not guess a conversion between P04 and P05 or delete a row automatically.
3. Check permission to read, copy and upload separately; explain why removing names does not guarantee anonymity and list the minimum fields needed.
4. Distinguish backup, working copy and suggestion output; describe human review of the five rows and fields, and accurately record unperformed actions.

There is no need for a real spreadsheet, personal information, sign-in, upload, formula or calculation exercise.

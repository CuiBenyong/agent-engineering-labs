# One suggested checklist and branch walkthrough

All instructions and statuses are fictional. Initially no real action has occurred. “To assess” means a condition is unknown, not “not applicable.”

| Action and order | Source | Condition and type | Initial applicability | Initial action status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Check availability | S1 | Required first step when intending to borrow | Applies to the fictional intention | Not performed |
| Place card and follow prompts to attempt borrowing | S2 | After the screen shows available | To assess | Not performed |
| Record the return date on the screen | S2 | After the screen shows borrowing succeeded | To assess | Not performed; date unknown |
| Ask staff; do not continue self-service before guidance | S3 | If the screen shows unavailable | To assess | Not performed |
| Ask staff; do not continue self-service before guidance | S3 | If the borrowing operation shows failure | To assess | Not performed |
| Tell staff and follow guidance; do not repair it yourself | S4 | If damage is noticed before return | To assess | Not performed |
| Hand the book to the service desk | S5 | At return when the book is not damaged | To assess | Not performed |
| Save the borrowing receipt | S6 | Optional after success if the reader wants it | Condition and preference to assess | Not performed |

**Hypothesis A: available, borrowing succeeded, no damage; the date is unrecorded, the book not handed over and the receipt choice undecided.** In this paper walkthrough, checking and the card-and-borrowing operation can be marked “hypothetically completed.” The unavailable and failure rows are each “hypothetically not applicable,” not completed inquiries. The damage branch is not applicable, not completed damage handling. Recording the date and handing over the undamaged book apply, but neither action was performed. The specific date remains unknown. Saving a receipt is optional after success and the reader has not decided.

**Hypothesis B: unavailable or operation failure.** If the screen shows unavailable, the corresponding S3 row applies: ask first and do not continue self-service before guidance; S2's available operation does not apply. If the screen first shows available and the attempted operation then shows failure, S3's failure row applies with the same ask-and-wait instruction. The success prerequisites for recording a date and saving a receipt were not met in that attempt. If the inquiry has not actually happened in either scenario, mark it not performed, not completed.

The source gives no specific return date, loan-period length or staff response time; do not add “fourteen days” or “ten minutes.” “To assess” means the condition is not established; “not applicable” means the relevant condition is known to be false; “not performed” means an action has not occurred. None means completed. No real borrowing, return or message occurred in this exercise.

# Formula review practice

In a fictional sheet, B is unit price, C is quantity and D is amount. Rows 2 to 4 contain 12.5 and 2, 8 and 3, and 10 and 0. Calculate the row amounts and total on paper, then explain the single-row and total references. Compare an incorrect total that omits row 3, and record a blank-value question and a privacy check. No real data upload or software execution is required.

# Self-introduction fact card and review

Practice with Chen He, a fictional applicant for a fictional bookstore assistant role. Duties include helping customers find books, organizing shelves and recording information, and using a checkout system according to store procedures. Fact card:

- **E1:** Volunteered at a community reading event, organized books, and helped visitors find the shelves.
- **E2:** Used a spreadsheet to record book titles and loan dates.
- **E3:** Has not used a checkout system.
- **E4:** Is willing to learn store procedures.
- **Not supplied:** school and qualifications, an employer or paid bookstore employment, number of visitors served, sales, or other results.

The following fictional incorrect draft is for review; it is not an actual AI output or real work history:

> Hello, I am Chen He. I managed a well-known bookstore, am proficient with checkout systems, and increased store sales by 30%. My university education guarantees I can do the job.

Inspect the draft and complete `starter/answer.md`: choose relevant actions, separate the known gap from missing information, and label each introduction sentence with its supporting fact. Rehearse naturally and record actual observations; if you cannot, write “not yet rehearsed.” Compare with `solution/answer.md`, which does not claim a completed rehearsal.

Paper is enough. No real résumé, identity document, or recording is required. The person and experience are fictional and must not be presented as your own work history.

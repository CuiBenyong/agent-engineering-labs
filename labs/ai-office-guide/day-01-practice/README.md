# Practice instructions

Use fictional material with paper or an authorized assistant. Do not upload private information.

Public material is organized; the venue and attendance remain unconfirmed.

Write a clear request, separate facts from open questions, inspect the draft and correct unsupported details. Compare the starter with the sample solution. Different accurate answers are welcome.

## Steps

1. Copy fictional note N1: Lin completed a poster first draft and collected two public venue descriptions; the time and venue were undecided and participants had not been counted.
2. Copy fictional meeting note M1: Lin was assigned to check venue availability Friday and Zhou to provide approved wording next Monday; exact dates and task outcomes need checking.
3. In the starter write a request, then draft completed work, open questions and next steps, a three-slide outline and an action table. Use paper or an approved assistant.
4. Compare each statement against N1 and M1. Remove unsupported numbers and keep venue and task outcomes unconfirmed. Compare the solution, record your corrections and keep the drafts locally without sending them.

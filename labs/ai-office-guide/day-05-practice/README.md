# Practice: a three-slide briefing and weekly update draft

Use paper, a document or an approved assistant. All material is fictional; do not connect email or send the work.

- N1: Lin completed a first poster draft and collected two public venue descriptions; at D1 the event time and venue were undecided and participants were not counted.
- M1: In a Wednesday discussion, Lin was assigned to check venue availability Friday.
- M2: Zhou was assigned to provide approved promotional wording next Monday.
- M3: The event time was discussed but no date was decided.
- M4: The venue is unconfirmed; two public venue descriptions were collected.
- M5: The notes do not state an approved budget or whether the wording is approved.
- M6: Someone proposed an entrance sign; adoption, owner and deadline were not stated.
- T1: The separate later D3 fictional simulation has planned/actual records of In person 40/50, Online 30/24 and Phone 10/16. Planned total 80, actual total 90, difference 10; completion 112.5%, or 12.5% above plan. The unit is records and unique people are unknown; this does not mean the count existed at D1.

## Steps

1. In the starter, outline three slides: known progress, later simulated data, and open questions and actions. Cite sources.
2. Draft the weekly update, keeping the D1/later-simulation time boundary, record unit and relative deadlines.
3. Trace at least six claims, check figures, timing and status, and correct the examples in the starter.
4. Compare the solution. You may use the D4 A1:C4 chart after checking its legend and numbers. Label the work “fictional practice, unsent.”

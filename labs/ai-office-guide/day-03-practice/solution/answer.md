# Sample: entry and check record

Keep separate source and working copies. Each row is a channel; B is planned record count and C is actual record count. Compare In person 40/50, Online 30/24 and Phone 10/16 cell by cell. Number cells contain numbers rather than unit text. The given A1:C4 has no blanks and each channel label appears once, but that does not prove person-level deduplication.

Example correction: “Working C3 was entered as 42; the exercise gives 24; changed to 24 and rechecked the row.” Preserve a real source and document corrections. A blank in a real source is not automatically zero; ask what it means. In this exercise, a missed B4 can be restored to 10 because the supplied input states it.

The aggregate table has no person-level detail or matching rule, so the unique-person count is unknown. Flag a repeated channel row, check its source and scope, and do not automatically delete or combine it.

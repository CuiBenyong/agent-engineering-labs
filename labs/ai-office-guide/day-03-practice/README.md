# Practice: enter and inspect simulated registration records

These figures are a separate fictional input introduced after D1. They do not imply that a count existed in D1 and are not unique-person counts. Use a spreadsheet or paper.

| Cell | Entry | Cell | Entry | Cell | Entry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Channel | B1 | Planned records | C1 | Actual records |
| A2 | In person | B2 | 40 | C2 | 50 |
| A3 | Online | B3 | 30 | C3 | 24 |
| A4 | Phone | B4 | 10 | C4 | 16 |

## Steps

1. Start a blank sheet and enter A1:C4 cell by cell, using numbers only in number cells. If no software is available, draw the grid on paper.
2. Save or copy a “source” version and a separate “working” version. Note that the unit is records and the unique-person count is unknown.
3. Compare rows and columns with the table above. Inspect headers, numeric cells, blanks, omissions and suspected repeated rows.
4. Complete the starter check record. Preserve original content and check suspected duplicates before deciding what to do. Compare the solution.

# Practice: calculate cells and inspect a chart

Use paper or a common spreadsheet application. The data are a separate fictional simulation introduced after D1. The unit is records and the unique-person count is unknown.

| Row | A | B | C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Channel | Planned records | Actual records |
| 2 | In person | 40 | 50 |
| 3 | Online | 30 | 24 |
| 4 | Phone | 10 | 16 |

## Steps

1. Open or build a working copy. Enter “Total” in A5 and “Difference (records)” in D1.
2. Enter the three difference formulas and three total formulas in the starter, cell by cell. On paper, write formulas beside the cells.
3. Check each row and total by hand, then verify the difference as actual total minus planned total. Calculate completion and the amount above plan separately.
4. Select A1:C4 and insert a side-by-side bar or column chart, or draw three pairs of bars on paper. Check the legend, six values, unit, fictional status and unknown unique-person count. Menus vary by product and version.
5. Compare the solution and retain the source and calculation copies. No assistant needs to edit a file.

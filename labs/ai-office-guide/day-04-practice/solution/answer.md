By hand, 40+30+10=80, 50+24+16=90 and 10-6+6=10; another route is 90-80=10. Completion is 90/80=112.5%; the amount above plan is 10/80=12.5%.

Select A1:C4 for a chart with three channels and planned and actual series; exclude the total row and difference column. A suitable title is “Fictional registration records: planned and actual,” with records as the unit. If series are reversed, adjust rows and columns and recheck 40/50, 30/24 and 10/16. Ninety is a record count, not a unique-person count; the table gives no causes.

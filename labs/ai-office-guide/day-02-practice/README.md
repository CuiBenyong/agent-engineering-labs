# Practice: meeting notes to an unsent update

Use paper, a document or an approved assistant. All material is fictional; no real meeting record or email is needed.

## Source material

- N1: Lin completed a first poster draft and collected two public venue descriptions; the event time and venue were undecided, and participants were not counted at D1.
- M1: In a Wednesday discussion, Lin was assigned to check venue availability Friday.
- M2: Zhou was assigned to provide approved promotional wording next Monday.
- M3: The event time was discussed, but no specific date was decided.
- M4: The venue is unconfirmed; two public venue descriptions were collected earlier.
- M5: The notes do not state an approved budget or whether the wording is approved.
- M6: Someone proposed an entrance sign; adoption, owner and deadline were not stated.

## Steps and checks

1. In the starter, separate proposals, assigned actions and open questions, citing sources.
2. Fill task, owner, relative deadline, source and status; keep M6 as a proposal and do not guess an owner.
3. Use the starter request to draft an update. Compare claims such as “venue booked” and “budget rejected” with the notes.
4. Compare the solution. Keep exact dates and both action outcomes unknown; do not send the draft.

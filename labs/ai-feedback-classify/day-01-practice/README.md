# Fictional shop feedback classification

Complete `starter/answer.md` on paper or in a document, then compare `solution/answer.md`. Every statement and ID is fictional; no AI call, upload or customer contact is required. Do not upload real customer data to AI. Even without names, wording and other details can identify someone.

## Scenario statements

The scope is only the following six fictional statements. No real time period, channel, sampling method or customer count is given.

| ID | Original wording |
| --- | --- |
| F01 | The corner of the package was crushed. |
| F02 | The cup is quite light and convenient to hold. |
| F03 | The price is a little high, but the cup feels comfortable to hold. |
| F04 | The wrapping paper was torn and the cup handle is loose. |
| F05 | It's all right, I suppose. |
| F06 | The delivery arrived one day later than promised. |

Use four exercise topics: packaging covers box or wrapping materials; product covers the cup's features, use experience or product problems; price covers price statements; delivery covers transport or arrival. A statement may receive multiple topics. If the subject is unclear, do not guess: mark the status “needs clarification.” The status is not a fifth topic.

## Four steps

1. Preserve each statement and fill topics, status and a separate inference or open-question column. F03 and F04 may have multiple topics; do not force a topic for F05.
2. Write a reusable request that uses only the fictional statements and asks for original wording, separated inferences and human review.
3. List IDs and counts for each topic. Separately record six statements, the number of topic assignments and the pending count; explain the scope and the effect of multiple labels.
4. Describe an item-by-item human review, permissions to check in real work, and causes, purchase intentions or customer counts that cannot be inferred.

## Completion criteria

Packaging is F01/F04, product is F02/F03/F04, price is F03 and delivery is F06, giving seven topic assignments. Five statements have topics and F05 needs clarification, giving six statements in all. Preserve F03's contrast and both observations in F04. Do not infer a transport cause or claim that six statements represent six real customers. All material is fictional.

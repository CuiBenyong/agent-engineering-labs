# Fictional mug product-image review

Complete `starter/answer.md` on paper or in a document, then compare `solution/answer.md`. No image generation, upload, account or payment is required. The product, people, permissions and images are fictional. The sample is an exercise in judgment, not a record of a real review.

## Given material

- Fictional specification F1: one cream ceramic mug with a handle; stated capacity 300 mL; mug height 9 cm; the sale includes one mug and no lid; no brand logo. The precise handle, rim and base shapes, heat tolerance and microwave suitability are not provided.
- Asset A: suppose photographer Lin permits a fictional photo on the shop's product detail page; upload to an image service and advertising use are unconfirmed. No actual photo or permission document is supplied.
- Asset B: suppose a competitor's image was found online without a rights source. Asset C: a plain background is planned as an original creation, but no actual file exists yet.
- Intended use: a promotional product illustration for a phone-viewed shop; the exact placement, dimensions and its rules are not yet known.
- Imaginary image A: a lid and the words “keeps drinks hot all day” appear. Imaginary image B: one lidless mug and no text, but the handle shape was invented by the model. These are descriptions only; no images have been generated.

## Four steps

1. List the facts supported by F1 and their source, separately from at least three open questions.
2. Complete a brief with intended use, the image's role, product requirements, exclusions, asset status and hold conditions.
3. Decide how to handle assets A and B and the plan for C. For each imaginary image, choose “revise and review again” or “hold,” explain why and say what evidence is needed.
4. Review the proposed words “stated capacity 300 mL” and “keeps drinks hot all day.” List pre-use checks for product measurements, image specifications, cropping, service terms and AI labeling.

## Completion criteria

Each product fact traces to F1; unknown details are not invented. Permissions are recorded for the specific uses. The unsupported claim and extra lid are caught, an unverified shape is not described as an actual product photograph, and image specifications remain pending until the destination is known. The final decision takes asset rights, copy, the image and applicable labeling requirements into account.

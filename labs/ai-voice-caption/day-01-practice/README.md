# Voice and caption review practice

Make a paper plan for a completely fictional reading-event clip. The three practice lines are:

1. Open a book, and let's talk about what we've been reading.
2. This fictional reading-event clip is for practicing voice and captions.
3. You can choose a book and talk about it with a friend.

In `starter/answer.md`, choose the audience, narration, and caption needs. Give the three lines a hypothetical timeline and explain how you would later check pronunciation, synchronization, and viewing without sound. Record source and permission checks for voice, music, and pictures. Without a real clip, all times are assumptions: write “not yet listened” and “synchronization not yet verified” rather than inventing results. When authorized material is available, adjust against the actual sound and review again.

Paper and a pen are enough; tools and media are optional. Do not clone another person's voice without explicit permission. You do not need private material or payment. Compare with `solution/answer.md`; other answers that meet the same conditions can work.

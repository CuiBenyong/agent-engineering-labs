# Sample: Venue inquiry email

Subject: Inquiry about a community reading exchange venue.

Hello, we are considering a community reading exchange where residents can share favorite books. Does your venue accept this kind of activity? Which times might be available, and how can we learn about fees and conditions? Thank you.

Separate hypothetical reply: “Some times next week may be available; the fee needs checking.” A follow-up could ask which times might work and who can confirm the fee. There is no actual reply, hold, price or sent message.

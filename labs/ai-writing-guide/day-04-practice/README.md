# Venue inquiry email

Fictional source: a community reading exchange is being considered so residents can share favorite books. The date, venue availability and fee are unconfirmed; no real contact or reply is supplied.

Use paper or a phone or computer note. An AI assistant is optional, and sending a real message is not part of the exercise.

1. List unresolved recipient, time, fee and condition questions.
2. Draft an unsent inquiry with a subject, context, two or three questions and a polite close. Do not use a real address or promise a booking.
3. Label this assumption separately: “Some times next week may be available; the fee needs checking.” Draft a follow-up asking for specific times and fee information.
4. Explain why the assumption is not an actual reply. Review and save without sending.

Complete starter/answer.md before comparing solution/answer.md. Different wording is fine when it is supported and preserves status.

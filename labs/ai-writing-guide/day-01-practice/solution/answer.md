# Sample answer

Known: The reading event date, venue and cost are not confirmed.

Request: Produce a short draft using only the known facts. Do not add unsupported dates, numbers or promises; mark missing information as unconfirmed.

Review: Compare each statement with the source. Keep unconfirmed details pending rather than presenting them as completed.

## Three sample drafts

Invitation: A community reading exchange is being considered. Residents are welcome to share favorite books; date, venue, fee and participation arrangements need confirmation.

Status: The reading event has been discussed; the venue still needs to be asked about, the promotional image is being made, and the date is undecided.

Email subject: Inquiry about a reading exchange venue. Hello, we are considering a community reading exchange. Does your venue accept this kind of activity, and how can we learn about possible times and fees? Thank you.

Correction: “Free this Saturday” lacks support. Remove both claims and keep date and fee pending. These practice drafts are unsent.

Use fictional material with paper or an authorized assistant. Do not upload private information.

The reading event date, venue and cost are not confirmed.

Write a clear request, separate facts from open questions, inspect the draft and correct unsupported details. Compare the starter with the sample solution. Different accurate answers are welcome.

## Complete the exercise

1. On paper or in a phone or computer note, list the reader, purpose and facts; date, venue, fee and registration remain pending.
2. Using fictional material, draft an invitation, internal status update and venue inquiry. Work on paper or with an authorized assistant; do not enter private information.
3. Identify an unsupported or ambiguous phrase, explain it and repair it; keep the image in progress and the venue unconfirmed.
4. Review and save all three drafts without sending. Compare with the sample; your wording may differ.

# Reading illustration revision and comparison

Complete `starter/answer.md` on paper or in a document, then compare `solution/answer.md`. Both versions are written scenarios, not actual pictures or tool-test results. No generation, upload, account or payment is needed.

## Given scenario

- Purpose: plan an illustrative image for a fictional reading-club group announcement, with room in the upper right for two short title lines.
- V1: soft watercolor style; a window on the left with warm light; on a wooden table, an open book at the lower left and a blue cup to the book's right. Plant leaves reach into the upper right; the book's bottom is cut off by the frame and the clear space in the corner is insufficient.
- This round's goals: the complete book remains lower left with a little space below it; about the upper-right quarter is clear.
- Imaginary V2: the book remains lower left and is complete with space below and about the upper-right quarter is clear; the cup remains to the right of the book but turns red, and the light turns cool. Assume the left window and watercolor style remain. No V3 is supplied.

## Four steps

1. Record at least three things to preserve and two problems in V1, using words or a sketch to show position.
2. Write a copyable request addressing only book placement and upper-right space. Name the things to preserve and how to check the result.
3. Compare V1 and imaginary V2 for each requested change and preservation condition. Decide whether V2 is a final image, then draft a next request based on V2. Do not fill in an unsupplied V3 result.
4. Set a stopping point and list permission, privacy, service-condition and labeling checks for upload and public use.

## Completion criteria

The request names two observable changes and preserves the blue cup, warm light and other scene elements. The comparison catches both improvements and both changes to cup color and light; it explains why the whole image needs another check after a revision. Label paper conclusions as imaginary and do not claim to have seen or used the image.

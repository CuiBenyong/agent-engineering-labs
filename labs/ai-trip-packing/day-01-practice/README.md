# Two-day packing and return review

A, B and all arrangements are fictional. They plan a two-day, one-night activity with one indoor activity during each day. The exercise gives no actual location, date, transport mode, weather, accommodation facilities, documents, personal medication or carrying rules; do not guess them.

For the quantity exercise, assume each person chooses two tops, two pieces of underwear, one sleepwear set and one personal toothbrush across the two days. Each is already wearing one top and one piece of underwear at departure. Each has a device requiring charging. They are discussing whether to share one cable, but connections, charging times, both people's willingness and the carrier are unconfirmed. Each person may decide whether to bring a small notebook.

Complete `starter/answer.md`: record each person's total, worn and planned-to-pack quantities; mark the exercise's essential, optional, proposed shared and unconfirmed items; list questions about weather, facilities, documents, medication and carrying rules; and make a return checklist for personal and shared belongings. No actual packing, booking or return check has occurred. Compare `solution/answer.md` when finished.

Success criteria: each person plans to pack one top and one piece of underwear; the two-person top total is four, with two worn and two planned for bags. Sleepwear is one set each and toothbrushes one each. Keep the cable proposed and unconfirmed, without declaring a carrier has agreed. Do not guess unknown requirements or mark return items as actually checked. Paper and pen are enough; no real names, addresses, documents or health data are needed.

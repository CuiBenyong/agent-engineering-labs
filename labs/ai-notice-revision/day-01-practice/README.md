# Compare notice revisions with the original

Complete `starter/answer.md` on paper or in a document, then compare `solution/answer.md`. The original, flawed revision and scenario are fictional. No AI call, upload, message sending or real owner approval is required or performed.

## Fictional original notice

> Borrowing in Area A of the records room will be paused on 14 November 2030 from 14:00 to 16:00. People who reserved Area A materials and have received a “materials ready” notice may collect the materials they reserved from Area A on the same day between 13:00 and 13:30. Area B is outside the scope of this adjustment. The contact method for questions and whether someone else may collect on a person's behalf have not been confirmed. The draft has not been confirmed by the responsible person and has not been sent.

This original is an unconfirmed practice draft, not an actual records-room arrangement.

## Imaginary flawed revision

“The entire records room is closed all day on 14 November 2030. Everyone may collect materials from any area between 13:00 and 14:00, without waiting for a ready notice; collection by another person is allowed. Call a fixed phone number for questions. The owner has approved this, so send it immediately.” This was written for practice, not observed as model output.

## Four steps

1. Mark the date, time and place of the borrowing pause; collection time, location, audience and both prerequisites; Area B's scope, open questions and unsent status.
2. Compare the flawed version item by item, identifying expanded scope, missing conditions, unknown details turned into facts and invented approval.
3. Write a reusable revision request and an unsent internal draft that preserves the meaning and all unconfirmed states.
4. List questions for the responsible person and state that no actual AI call, approval or sending occurred in this exercise.

## Completion criteria

The draft preserves Area A's borrowing pause on 14 November 2030 from 14:00 to 16:00. Collection is that day from 13:00 to 13:30 in Area A for people who reserved Area A materials and received a ready notice, covering the materials they reserved. Area B is outside this adjustment. The contact method, proxy collection and owner confirmation remain open; the draft is unsent. Do not treat “materials they reserved” as permission or prohibition for proxy collection.

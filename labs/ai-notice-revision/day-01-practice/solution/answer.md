Area B is outside the scope of this adjustment. The contact method for questions and whether someone else may collect on a person's behalf have not been confirmed. The draft has not been confirmed by the responsible person and has not been sent.

- Collection: in Area A that day from 13:00 to 13:30, covering materials the person reserved. Both prerequisites apply: reserved Area A materials **and** received a ready notice.
- Area B is outside this adjustment. That does not establish all-day opening or normal operation of every service in Area B.
- Contact method and proxy collection are unconfirmed. The responsible person has not confirmed the draft and it has not been sent. Ownership of a reservation does not settle proxy permission.

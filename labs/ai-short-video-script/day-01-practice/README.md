# Short-video shot-list practice

Complete the starter for a fictional neighborhood reading event: audience, purpose, confirmed and pending information, equipment and target duration. Draft four shots, add their estimated durations, identify one unconfirmed or unavailable detail, and propose a revision. Use a suitable text assistant or work on paper. If you did not generate or film anything, say so.

Compare with the solution. No private information or payment is needed; other solutions that meet the brief are welcome.

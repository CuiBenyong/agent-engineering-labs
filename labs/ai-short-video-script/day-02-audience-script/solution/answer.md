# One suggested answer

Audience: adults interested in reading discussion. Purpose: introduce reading exchange. Date, place and signup are pending. Hook: “Would you like to talk with someone about a book you have read?” Follow with “A reading group can begin with a book and a thought,” “This is a fictional exercise; the exact format is pending,” and “Time, place and registration are to be confirmed.” These lines can map to four shots. The learner must record their own timing; if the read is long, remove repetition and try again rather than inventing a result. Verify the sharing format before real use.

This is a fictional example for comparison. Other answers can meet the brief. Verify facts and permissions before any real use.

Alternative opening: “Have you read a line you would like to discuss?” Choose the book question because it directly introduces reading exchange. Revision example: change “Come on Saturday and you will make friends” to “Would you like to talk with someone about a book you have read?” This removes an unconfirmed date and a promised outcome. Record your own read-through time or mark it unmeasured.

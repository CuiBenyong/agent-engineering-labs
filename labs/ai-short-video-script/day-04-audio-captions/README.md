## Task

Choose audio you are authorized to use; plan the recording setting and a retake; write captions for four time ranges; draw a static timeline with image, audio and caption rows; record readability checks.

## Steps

1. Open starter/answer.md and copy its fields to paper or a local note. Complete each field.
2. Use only the fictional course scenario: adults interested in reading discussion, a phone and tabletop materials; date, place and registration are pending.
3. A text assistant is optional; paper works throughout. No private uploads, payment, filming or publication are needed.
4. Compare your work with solution/answer.md for facts, timing and permission. Explain any different choice.
5. State what remains unverified and distinguish a plan from something that actually happened.

## Acceptance

Complete every starter field, explain one revision and identify one outstanding check. For timing or reading speed, use your own measurement.

# Spoken script and rehearsal log

Practice with a completely fictional shared-bookcase event. Known facts: it takes place on Saturday from three to four in the afternoon in the community multipurpose room; participants may voluntarily bring one book to share or come without one; a book brought to the event does not have to be given away. No calendar date, street address, cost, registration process, or contact has been supplied. Do not publish it as a real event.

Write for neighbors who may enjoy reading. The total limit is 60 seconds, and the tone should sound like a conversation with a neighbor. Complete the fact card, gaps, and first draft in `starter/answer.md`. Time two natural readings and record actual seconds and stumbling points. Revise, read the whole new version, and time it again. Account separately for any opening or ending outside the timed read, then check facts, privacy, and permissions. If you cannot speak or time a reading yet, finish the writing and mark it “not yet rehearsed”; return later rather than inventing seconds.

Paper and a pen are enough; an AI text tool is optional. Do not upload real personal data or unauthorized material. Compare your work with `solution/answer.md`. Other answers can work, and the suggested answer does not prove your delivery fits the limit.

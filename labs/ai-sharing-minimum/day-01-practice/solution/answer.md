# Sample Answer: Decide the Information Scope First

## Goal and Output

Demonstrate a blank schedule table for a wholly fictional community craft event. Provide headings and two invented requirement rows. Do not assign people or decide whether a real event is fully staffed. If headings alone were requested, even the example times and numbers could be omitted.

| Type of field | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fictional time, role, proposed headcount | Use independently invented A and B values | Demonstrate how to enter requirements |
| Names and contact details | Omit | The layout does not contact or assign individuals |
| Personal restrictions and notes | Do not collect or provide | The task does not judge individual circumstances |
| Real date, location, list | Omit | The example does not require a real event's details |
| Confirmed attendance and decisions | Unknown; do not fill in | The fictional setup supplies no such facts |

## Fictional Request

Please design a blank schedule table for a wholly fictional community craft event and demonstrate two rows: 09:00–10:00 check-in, proposed headcount 2; 10:00–11:00 guiding visitors, proposed headcount 1. Show only time, role, and proposed headcount. Do not assign people or guess names, contact details, or personal circumstances. Label the example as fictional and identify what cannot be established from it.

The request stays on paper. The independently invented example cannot substitute for a real schedule and does not prove that any dataset is anonymous.

## Check the Simulated Answer

“Both shifts have been staffed” has no support: proposed places are not attendance. “The list is completely anonymous” has not been proven; deleting names does not guarantee that other combinations cannot identify someone. “Invitations have already been sent” is unsupported, and this exercise did not send any.

One acceptable example:

| Time slot | Role | Proposed headcount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 09:00–10:00 | Check-in | 2 |
| 10:00–11:00 | Guiding visitors | 1 |

Each row lasts one hour. The sum, 2 + 1 = 3, is three role-slot places; it does not mean three distinct people or establish anyone's attendance. The event, arrangements, and headcounts are fictional or proposed and should not be used for real decisions.

## Combinations and Status

Even without a name, a hypothetical unusual role, precise time, and personal arrangement might combine with other knowledge to narrow a record to a person. Whether identification is possible depends on context. A blank table needs no such individual records. The exercise supplies no real special role or personal information.

- This paper exercise: **not shared**; nothing was uploaded or sent.
- Separate hypothetical in which a real submission occurred: **already shared**. Later local deletion does not automatically withdraw it. Record known channel, scope, and time within your authority and contact the relevant responsible person if needed. Do not claim the recipient deleted or never viewed it.
- Submission uncertain: **unconfirmed**. Check actual records available to you; do not send the material again.

Real arrangements, attendance, coverage, sharing permission, and the specific outcome of any past sharing cannot be established by this exercise.

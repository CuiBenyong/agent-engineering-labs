# Choose Fields Before Sharing

Allow about 35 minutes and use paper and a pen. The event and numbers were invented for the exercise. Do not collect real personal information, upload, or send any materials.

## Task

Design a blank schedule table for a fictional community craft event and demonstrate how to enter two independently invented requirements:

| Label | Time slot | Role | Proposed headcount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 09:00–10:00 | Check-in | 2 |
| B | 10:00–11:00 | Guiding visitors | 1 |

A proposed headcount does not count actual sign-ups or attendance. No date, location, list of people, or approved arrangement is given. The output demonstrates headings and two rows; it does not assign anyone or describe a real event.

## Steps

1. Record the goal in `starter/answer.md`, choose fields to use or omit, and explain why. Consider which invented values could also be left out if only headings were required.
2. Write a request using only the values invented on this page. Label the example fictional and the headcounts proposed; prohibit guesses about individuals. Do not copy values from a real list. A fictional example cannot determine a real schedule.
3. Check the simulated answer: “Both shifts have been staffed, the list is completely anonymous, and invitations have already been sent.” Explain what evidence is missing for each claim, and show acceptable headings and the two sample rows.
4. Explain why an unusual role, precise time, and personal arrangement might be linked even without a name. Do not supply real distinctive information or promise either anonymity or identification.
5. Record the sharing status of this paper exercise. In a **separate hypothetical situation**, assume material was actually submitted: how would you record its status after deleting a local copy? What if you were unsure whether it was submitted?

Afterward, consult `solution/answer.md`. It is one possible response; facts and statuses must match the exercise. Do not present a hypothetical as an event that occurred.

Minimal handoff example for C3: the customer asks about a refund and its timing. The fact card gives no refund policy or timeline; H1 only says staff handle this case in the exercise. Staff need to check the conditions and timing. The intended owner is an employee authorized to handle refunds. The actual status is **awaiting handoff**. Include no real order ID, name, address or payment information. C2, C4 and C5 also remain awaiting handoff; writing this record does not mean an employee has accepted them.

If a customer describes an immediate personal-safety emergency, follow the local emergency process and seek urgent help instead of putting the case in an ordinary support queue. For a real handoff, use an organization-approved channel and let authorized staff perform verification. Before sending, check evidence, scope, promises, recipient and privacy.

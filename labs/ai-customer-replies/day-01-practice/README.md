# Shop fact card and human-handoff practice

“Meadow Shop,” the customer questions and the fact card are fictional. They contain no real shop policy or customer data:

- F1: In-store collection is available Monday through Friday, 10:00–18:00; other times are not stated.
- F2: Paper-bag packaging is offered; the price and other packaging are not stated.
- H1: In this exercise, staff handle refunds, allergies, order disputes and questions outside the fact card. This is not a universal routing rule for all businesses.

Five fictional questions:

1. C1: “When can I collect in store?”
2. C2: “Is there a charge for a paper bag?”
3. C3: “I want a refund. When will it arrive?”
4. C4: “I'm allergic to peanuts. Can I eat this food?”
5. C5: “There is a problem with my order and I think the handling was unfair.”

Complete `starter/answer.md`: give a classification, source, draft for review and open questions for each. Make a minimal handoff record for issues needing a person, marking that no case has actually been submitted. Check that the drafts invent no policy, price, outcome or timeline, contain no real customer data and do not claim to have been sent. Compare `solution/answer.md`; you may use your own wording.

Success criteria: C1's draft is supported by F1; C2–C5 are for a human under H1 with their distinct unknowns preserved; at least one handoff identifies the issue, source, unknowns, kind of owner and “awaiting handoff” status; all drafts remain unsent. Paper and pen are enough. There is no need to sign in, upload information or contact anyone.

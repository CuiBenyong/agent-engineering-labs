# Notebook product-copy source review

Complete `starter/answer.md` on paper or in a document, then compare `solution/answer.md`. No AI call, upload, account or payment is required. All products, records and drafts are fictional; they are not actual testing, legal review or publication records.

## Scenario records

- F1 product specification: A5, 148 by 210 mm; light-brown cover; ruled inner pages; 80 pages counting both sides.
- F2 use note: intended for everyday handwritten notes.
- F3 sales contents: one notebook per sale; no pen included.
- The exercise assumes a fictional responsible person checked these records. It does not provide water resistance, bleed-through, environmental material, price, promotion, stock or shipping details. Unknown is neither yes nor no.
- Imaginary flawed draft: “This A5 notebook has an eco-friendly kraft cover, never bleeds through with any pen and doubles your productivity. Lowest price anywhere: 9.9, with a free pen in every order.” This was written for practice; it is not observed model output.

## Four steps

1. Use F1, F2 and F3 to list supported specifications, use and sales contents, and at least three unknowns.
2. Split the flawed draft into six claims: A5, eco-friendly kraft cover, never bleeds, doubled productivity, lowest price 9.9 and free pen. Record evidence and a decision to retain, remove, hold or correct each one.
3. Write a product-page title and description plus a short community post. Add a supporting source ID to every sentence and use only what the scenario supports.
4. Review units, page-count conditions, pen inclusion, price and promotion across both channels. List checks for source version, permission, presentation and human review before use.

## Completion criteria

A5 traces to F1; unsupported material, performance, effect and price claims are removed or held. Correct the free-pen claim because it conflicts with F3. Both channel drafts trace each sentence to a source, leave unknowns open and retain conditions needed for understanding. This is general content review only.

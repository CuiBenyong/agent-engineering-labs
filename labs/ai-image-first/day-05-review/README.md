# D5 practice: Use checklist and reflection

## Steps

1. Select your D4 file or paper layout and record its version, actual status and intended use.
2. Check content accuracy, privacy, cost, permissions for background, font and other material, AI disclosure, and destination conditions separately.
3. Mark each confirmed, needs change, or to confirm, with actual evidence. Mark unused material not applicable.
4. Choose continue, revise or pause. Give each unknown an actionable next step and completion condition.
5. In three sentences describe the need, evidence gaps and where to start next. Do not invent observations or labels when there is no generated image.

## Acceptance

Every check has a status and evidence or reason for uncertainty; the decision follows the next actions; the three reflections are specific and honest. The solution is a paper example, not permission or legal advice.

# D2 practice: Compare two prompts

## Steps

1. In the starter, identify the book and tea, window table, upper clear space, and warm hand-drawn style.
2. Write two complete requests with shared conditions; change only the main placement from lower center to lower right.
3. Draw two paper sketches or, after reviewing permissions, attempts and cost, choose a suitable tool. Record whether you actually generated anything.
4. Compare subject, upper clear space, portrait use, and unwanted people, marks or writing for both. Mark actual images as awaiting inspection if none exists.
5. Choose a version, explain why, and record one issue to revisit in D3.

## Acceptance

Both prompts stand alone; their main difference is explicit; all four comparison rows have a status; the choice and next issue are specific. Predictions are labeled, not reported as observations. The solution is a paper example; other sound answers work.

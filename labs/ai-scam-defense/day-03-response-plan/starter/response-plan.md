# Day 3 worksheet: three hypotheses and a response card

Use fictional details only and work on paper. Do not write actual codes, identifying details beyond the fictional names, account details, or private phone numbers. Do not actually contact anyone, call, pay, access an account, download or install software, click suspicious links, or upload real materials. A, B, and C are separate hypotheses; see the [lab instructions](../README.md).

## Emergency check

If someone may be in immediate physical danger, I immediately contact this local emergency service via a trusted route: ___; I do not wait for: ___.

## A: a one-time code was disclosed

- Fictional known facts / remaining unknowns: ___ / ___
- What to stop providing or paying: ___
- Independent official bank or payment-provider channel, what I would report, and what account protection I would request: ___
- Independent route to local police; how a trusted person can help: ___ / ___
- What existing records can be kept safely without delaying help? ___

## B: payment was completed

- Fictional payment method, known facts, and unknowns: ___
- Stop further payments and disclosure: ___
- Which independent official bank or provider channel will I contact promptly? Which options will I ask about? ___
- How can I contact police promptly as appropriate locally, involve a trusted person, and keep available records without delay? ___
- Is stopping or recovering the payment guaranteed? Why? ___

## C: unfamiliar remote-access software was installed

- Fictional known facts / unknown exposure: ___ / ___
- How will I stop screen sharing and the other party's connection? When could I disconnect that device from the network? ___
- Which device should I use first, and which independent official channels reach the bank or payment provider and police? ___
- Whose guidance will I follow for device and account recovery? What should I not enter on a possibly controlled device? ___
- What can I retain safely without delaying help? Who can help? ___

## Response card (fictional plan only)

1. Immediate physical danger: ___
2. Stop further disclosure and payment: ___
3. Bank or payment provider and police: ___
4. Stop possible remote access; use another trusted device; follow recovery guidance: ___
5. Appropriate records and a trusted helper: ___
6. If someone promises recovery for an advance fee: ___
7. Remaining uncertainty and limits on the outcome: ___

In mainland China, which numbers are for police reporting and emergency medical assistance? What is 96110 for, and what does it not replace? ___

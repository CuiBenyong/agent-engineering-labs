# Choose and review a Moments image

Xiaoman is fictional. She wants to share only: “I'm planning to sort my old books this weekend and see which ones I want to read again.” She has not organized an event or announced an event location, event time, price or contact. Candidate A resembles a photo of a corner bookshop, with a name on the door and “free exchange on Sunday” beside a blurry string of numbers. Candidate B is a simple illustration of a stack of books and a cloth bag, with no readable shop name, people or event text. These are descriptions, not actual images.

Complete `starter/answer.md`: choose a candidate and explain whether its meaning fits the post; sketch B and use paper frames to consider two obstructions; record checks for facts, privacy, image permission and labels; decide whether to continue, revise and recheck, or hold, and name the next step. Label assumptions as anticipated checks and unsupported items as unconfirmed. Then compare `solution/answer.md`; other well-supported answers are welcome.

Success criteria:

1. Explain the choice and identify the unsupported implications in candidate A.
2. Record potential obstructions from the paper frames and state that they are not an actual Moments preview.
3. Distinguish given facts from unconfirmed content and address privacy, permission and labeling separately.
4. Give a decision consistent with the evidence, including unresolved items and a next step.

Paper and pen are enough. An optional tool trial depends on the current interface; you do not need to pay, upload a real photograph or publish a post.

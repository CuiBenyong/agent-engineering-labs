# Fictional pre-contact service questions

Complete `starter/answer.md` on paper or in a document, then compare `solution/answer.md`. The device, observation, date and provider are fictional. No equipment operation, AI call, upload, provider contact or booking is required.

For actual electrical safety, follow product instructions and trusted professional guidance. This exercise gives no disassembly, electrical-operation steps or fault diagnosis.

## Scenario

- O1: an old desk lamp; no make or model is supplied.
- O2: on 12 November 2030, the person previously observed the light flickering intermittently during use.
- O3: no additional checks or attempts were made; the cause is unknown.
- O4: no provider was contacted; there is no response, quote or booking.
- O5: the person wants to learn whether an inspection can be arranged before deciding whether to book.

Imaginary flawed draft: “The provider has confirmed the cause of the lamp's fault. A free inspection is available on Saturday morning and is already booked. Give your name, phone number and home address to AI.” This was written for practice, not observed as a model or provider response.

## Four steps

1. List the past observation, unknown cause and current contact status without inventing a model or inspection result.
2. Write at least four pre-contact questions covering whether inspection is accepted, possible times, charges and information requirements.
3. Compare the flawed draft with the scenario, identifying unsupported claims about cause, time, fees, booking and personal information. Write a reusable AI request using fictional material only and an unsent inquiry.
4. Record privacy, safety and status: no contact or booking occurred and no real personal information is uploaded. Actual safety decisions need product instructions and trusted professional guidance.

## Completion criteria

Preserve the past intermittent flickering and date accurately, leaving the cause unknown. Service scope, timing, charges and information requirements remain questions. The unsent draft gives no diagnosis or electrical-operation steps and does not claim a booking.

Reference: [Electrical Safety Foundation appliance safety guidance](https://www.esfi.org/appliance-safety-tips/). For a real appliance, also consult applicable product instructions and local professional guidance.

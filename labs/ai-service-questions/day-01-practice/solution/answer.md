# Sample answer: pre-contact service inquiry

This is paper preparation for a fictional scenario. No real equipment inspection, AI call, provider contact, quote, booking or sending occurred. It supplies no fault diagnosis or electrical-operation instructions.

## 1. Fictional observation and status

- O1: an old desk lamp; no make or model supplied.
- O2: on 12 November 2030, the person previously observed the light flickering intermittently during use.
- O3: no additional checks or attempts were made; the cause is unknown and must not be invented.
- O4: no provider was contacted; there is no response, quote or booking.
- O5: the person wants to learn whether an inspection can be arranged before deciding whether to book.

## 2. Pre-contact questions

1. Do you accept this kind of old desk lamp for inspection?
2. If so, what times may be available and how is a time confirmed?
3. Is there an inspection charge, how is it determined and are other costs explained in advance?
4. Before deciding whether to book, what information is needed and how should it be provided?

These are unanswered questions. No provider has confirmed availability, free service or acceptance of the device.

## 3. Review the imaginary flawed draft

| Flawed claim | Scenario status | Revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provider confirmed the cause | No contact; cause unknown | Remove diagnosis and confirmation; retain the past observation |
| Free inspection available Saturday morning | Time and charges unknown | Ask separately about available times and fees |
| Booking complete | No contact or booking | State that it is unbooked; the person decides after any response |
| Give real name, phone and address to AI | No real personal information is needed | Do not upload it; the person verifies a trusted channel and necessity for any real need |

## 4. Draft and boundaries

Reusable request:

> Using the fictional old-lamp situation, preserve the past observation of intermittent flickering and date, unknown cause, no additional checks and no contact or booking. Prepare pre-contact questions about inspection acceptance, timing, charges and information required. Do not diagnose, provide disassembly or electrical-operation steps, invent a response or booking, upload material or send a message.

Unsent draft:

> Hello, I would first like to ask whether you offer inspections for an old desk lamp. On 12 November 2030 I previously observed the light flickering intermittently during use. I do not know the cause and have not made any additional checks. Do you accept this kind of device for inspection? If so, what times might be available, is there an inspection charge and how would the fee be confirmed? Before I decide whether to book, what information would you need and through what method should it be provided? I am only inquiring and have not booked. Thank you.

- Do not upload real names, phone numbers, addresses, order numbers, photos or other personal information to AI. If a real need arises later, the person first verifies the provider, channel and necessity.
- Actual electrical safety requires product instructions and trusted professional guidance. There is no need to repeat the observation or attempt a fix for this exercise.
- No real provider was contacted and there was no quote, booking, message or equipment inspection.

# One suggested comparison

Both versions and the reading room are fictional. A6 and B5 state that neither is approved or effective. This compares supplied text, not an actual policy decision.

| Topic | Version A | Version B | Change and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Period and date | A1: 14 days, no date | B1: 21 days, proposed May 1, 2031 | Figures differ by 7 days; the proposed date is added and does not prove effectiveness. |
| Per-person limit | A2: at most 2 books | B2: at most 2 books | Corresponding wording is unchanged, not every condition. |
| Library card | A3: required to borrow | B3: required; a person without one may first ask | Adds an inquiry path, not proof of borrowing without a card. |
| Renewal | A4: if needed, request before due date | B4: if needed, request at least two days before | Conditional group remains; request timing changes. The way to count days is unstated. |
| Paper form | A5: paper forms available | Not mentioned in B | Removed corresponding text does not establish cancellation of the service. |
| Draft status | A6: not approved or effective | B5: not approved or effective | Corresponding wording unchanged. |

The simulated summary's 7-day difference comes from 21 − 14; it does not prove B is effective. Renewal timing changed, so “all other conditions are unchanged” is wrong. Asking does not establish borrowing without a card, and omission does not establish canceled paper forms.

Ask: Will the proposed date be approved? How are the two days counted? May someone borrow after asking without a card? Are paper forms described elsewhere, and are both files complete? This exercise provides no answers, makes no legal judgment, and edits no document.

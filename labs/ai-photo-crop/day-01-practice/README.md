# Cropping practice

Imagine a portrait image 1,200 pixels wide and 1,600 high, with a book and cup in the middle and a delivery label near the top. Draw a centered four-to-three landscape crop using the full 1,200-pixel width. Record what to keep and what might be lost, calculate the frame height and the total height removed, then list checks before use. Work on paper; do not upload a real photo.
